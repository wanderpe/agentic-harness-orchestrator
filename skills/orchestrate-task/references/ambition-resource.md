# Optional outcome lens

Resource: [How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/) — Felix Rieseberg, October 13, 2026. Linked with a short original summary; the article is not redistributed.

The article distinguishes speed, altitude, depth and range: faster delivery, outcome-level thinking, stronger methods, and useful perspectives from other fields. It emphasizes raw evidence and outside validation over confident role-playing.

Harness application: for an ambiguous or difficult task, record the intended outcome, best available source evidence, a suitable method and an external success check. Add another discipline only when it changes the method or test. Keep stretch goals testable. Broader possibilities are suggestions until authorized; preserve scope, budgets, permissions and release gates. For routine work, skip this lens.
